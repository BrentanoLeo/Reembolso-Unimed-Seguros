Request a Reimbursement via the App

Step 1: Log in​

Open the Unimed Seguros app and enter your CPF and password.

Step 2: Access the Health section​

On the home screen, tap Health.

    Note: If the Reimbursements and Insurance shortcut is configured on the home screen, you can access it directly without navigating through the menu.

Step 3: Access the Reimbursements and Previews section​

In the Financial section, select Reimbursements and Previews.

Step 4: Tap the New Request button​

On the Reimbursements and Previews screen, any requests in progress will be displayed.

To start a new request, tap New Request.

Step 5: Access the Reimbursement section​

Tap Reimbursement to start a new reimbursement request.

Step 6: Complete facial recognition​

If prompted, allow camera access.

During this step, the app will open the device camera for facial recognition.

Position your face in the center of the screen and tap Start recognition. Follow the on-screen instructions until recognition is complete.

    Tip: Tap the camera flip icon to switch between the front and rear camera.

Step 7: Register a bank account (if needed)​

If no bank account has been registered yet, tap Register new account.

Fill in the required information and tap Continue.

Step 8: Enter the Procedure Type​

Tap Procedure Type and select the option that matches the procedure performed.

Step 9: Enter appointment details​

  1. Under Appointment type, select the type of consultation.

  2. Under ICD, enter or select the corresponding code.

  3. Under Sessions completed, enter the date and amount for each session.

  4. Tap Continue.

Step 10: Attach documents​

Attach the required documents:

  1. Proof of payment/receipt;

  2. Referral;

  3. Fiscal receipt.

If prompted, repeat the process in the optional documents section.

To attach a document, tap Attach next to each respective field and choose to take a photo or select an existing file.

After attaching all required documents, tap Continue.

Step 11: Enter provider details​

Fill in the required fields:

In Provider CPF or CNPJ, enter the service provider's identification number.

In Service Provider Name, type the provider's name.

In Council number, enter the corresponding professional council number.

In State, select the state where the appointment took place.

In ZIP code, enter the provider's ZIP code. If you do not have this information, tap I don't know my ZIP code. The app will open the postal service website where you can look up the provider's ZIP code. After looking it up, return to the app and complete the field.

After filling in all required fields, tap Continue.

Step 12: Enter payment details​

Fill in the required fields:

In Invoice Number, enter the invoice number provided by the service provider.

In Authentication Code (optional), enter the invoice authentication code, if available.

The Amount field is filled in automatically based on the information entered in previous steps.

In Issue date, select the invoice issue date.

After filling in all required fields, tap Continue.

Step 13: Confirm the details​

Review the information displayed on the screen.

If the bank account is incorrect, tap Use a different account. If the beneficiary is incorrect, select the correct beneficiary.

After confirming the information, tap Submit.

Step 14: Finish​

After submitting the request, the app will display the protocol number and the deadline for completing the review.

Save the protocol number for future reference.

The reimbursement request has been completed.
