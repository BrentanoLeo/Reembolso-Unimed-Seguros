## Introduction

This documentation was created to address a need identified while using the Unimed Seguros app as a plan beneficiary. Unimed Seguros is the healthcare and insurance platform adopted by SAP in São Leopoldo, serving more than 2,500 employees.

Given this volume of users, essential features — such as submitting reimbursement requests for consultations and procedures — must be easy to find and complete. However, the need to locate or enable certain options within the app can add complexity to the user journey and cause confusion during the process.

With that in mind, this documentation's primary goal is to make the reimbursement request feature more accessible and to guide users through the submission process in a practical, clear, and efficient way.

## Information Architecture

The reimbursement request process through the Unimed Seguros app follows a straightforward flow, which eliminated the need to create multiple pages or documents to cover it. Although the procedure involves 14 steps, all of them could be organized sequentially on a single page while preserving clarity and ease of navigation.

The documentation was structured to serve users with varying levels of familiarity with the app, adopting a procedural approach with objective instructions and clearly defined steps. Additionally, the content organization prioritizes elements and features that tend to remain stable over time, reducing the risk of obsolescence and contributing to the longevity of the material.

## Style

The document is structured in 14 sequential steps, referred to in this material as steps. Numbered 1 through 14, each step follows a consistent hierarchy composed of the following elements:

1. Step number and title
2. Instruction in imperative form (the action expected of the user)
3. Screenshot corresponding to the step

The use of sequential step numbering was intended to reinforce the user's sense of progress throughout the task and to make the process easier to follow. The numbering also allows the reader to quickly identify their position in the flow and estimate the effort required to complete it.

Instructions were written in the imperative form to clearly and objectively communicate the actions required to successfully submit a reimbursement request. This reduces interpretive ambiguity and increases the predictability of the interaction.

Screenshots required decisions related to visual hierarchy, highlighting of relevant elements, and reduction of cognitive load. Together, titles, text, and images were organized in a complementary way, allowing each component to serve a specific function in the learning and task execution process.

One key decision made during writing was to bold button names and to preserve them exactly as they appear in the app, including differences in capitalization.

This decision was made for the following reasons:

- Buttons are the primary navigation mechanism between steps and are responsible for guiding the user to the reimbursement request feature. For this reason, they received greater visual emphasis in the text.

- The naming defined by the app developers was preserved, even in the absence of a consistent capitalization standard. Faithfully reproducing the labels reduces the possibility of friction between the documentation and the actual interface.

- Examples of this decision include buttons such as "Reimbursements and Previews", "Reimbursement", and "Procedure Type", whose spelling differences were maintained to ensure a direct correspondence between the documentation and the experience encountered by the user.

- If standardizing labels were the responsibility of this documentation, it would be recommended to adopt a single naming convention throughout the entire interface, promoting greater visual and cognitive consistency.

Finally, screenshots were used as a visual confirmation mechanism for the written instructions, allowing the user to quickly validate their position in the flow and recognize the elements that require interaction at each step.
