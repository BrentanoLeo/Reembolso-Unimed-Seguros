# Request a Reimbursement via the App

## Step 1: Log in

Open the Unimed Seguros app and enter your CPF and password.

<img width="173" height="350" alt="Unimed Seguros app home screen before login." src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8aa367f6-0c68-4b57-ba33-61d8f21b9b02" />

## Step 2: Access the Health section

On the home screen, tap **Health**.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Note: If the **Reimbursements and Insurance** shortcut is configured on the home screen, you can access it directly without navigating through the menu.

<img width="173" height="350" alt="App home screen with the Health button available." src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f4d94723-d237-4e7f-8bee-270e76560fdc" />

## Step 3: Access the Reimbursements and Previews section

In the **Financial** section, select **Reimbursements and Previews**.

<img width="173" height="350" alt="Financial section showing the Reimbursements and Previews option." src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fbf365e4-2cae-4973-b130-9ca6dfd0635b" />

## Step 4: Tap the New Request button

On the **Reimbursements and Previews** screen, any requests in progress will be displayed.

To start a new request, tap **New Request**.

<img width="173" height="350" alt="Reimbursements and Previews screen showing existing requests and the New Request button." src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/262f5e47-d92e-443d-95d1-f9f9879d22b5" />

## Step 5: Access the Reimbursement section

Tap **Reimbursement** to start a new reimbursement request.

<img width="173" height="350" alt="New request screen with the Reimbursement option available." src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/368a2fb0-90ba-4e73-bbd3-94207eb58498" />

## Step 6: Complete facial recognition

If prompted, allow camera access.

During this step, the app will open the device camera for facial recognition.

Position your face in the center of the screen and tap **Start recognition**.
Follow the on-screen instructions until recognition is complete.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;Tip: Tap the camera flip icon to switch between the front and rear camera.

<img width="173" height="350" alt="Facial recognition screen with face framing guide and Start Recognition button." src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b2e1ae80-3ef3-4f68-ba7f-3472312db382" />

## Step 7: Register a bank account (if needed)

If no bank account has been registered yet, tap **Register new account**.

Fill in the required information and tap **Continue**.

<img width="173" height="350" alt="Screen for registering a new bank account with fields for account details." src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/385a1218-3d65-4ccc-8647-aba3e800837e" />

## Step 8: Enter the Procedure Type

Tap **Procedure Type** and select the option that matches the procedure performed.

<img width="173" height="350" alt="Procedure Type screen with available procedure options for selection." src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7effe105-d58c-47d7-af16-15a3cc9e81e5" />

## Step 9: Enter appointment details

1. Under Appointment type, select the type of consultation.

2. Under **ICD**, enter or select the corresponding code.

3. Under Sessions completed, enter the date and amount for each session.

4. Tap **Continue**.

<img width="173" height="350" alt="Appointment details screen with fields for appointment type, ICD code, and sessions completed." src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/514ff6a0-df32-4a37-a370-ce5a9614310d" />

## Step 10: Attach documents

Attach the required documents:

1. Proof of payment/receipt;

2. Referral;

3. Fiscal receipt.

If prompted, repeat the process in the optional documents section.

To attach a document, tap **Attach** next to each respective field and choose to take a photo or select an existing file.

After attaching all required documents, tap **Continue**.

<img width="173" height="350" alt="Document attachment screen with fields for proof of payment, referral, and fiscal receipt." src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/382d5e9d-e20e-4986-9c17-7c1d2fe0a07c" />

## Step 11: Enter provider details

Fill in the required fields:

In **Provider CPF or CNPJ**, enter the service provider's identification number.

In **Service Provider Name**, type the provider's name.

In **Council number**, enter the corresponding professional council number.

In **State**, select the state where the appointment took place.

In **ZIP code**, enter the provider's ZIP code.
If you do not have this information, tap **I don't know my ZIP code**. The app will open the postal service website where you can look up the provider's ZIP code. After looking it up, return to the app and complete the field.

After filling in all required fields, tap **Continue**.

<img width="173" height="350" alt="Provider details screen with fields for CPF or CNPJ, name, professional council number, state, and ZIP code." src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0b93a911-4e51-44f7-8241-188fc39b5a74" />

## Step 12: Enter payment details

Fill in the required fields:

In **Invoice Number**, enter the invoice number provided by the service provider.

In **Authentication Code (optional)**, enter the invoice authentication code, if available.

The **Amount** field is filled in automatically based on the information entered in previous steps.

In **Issue date**, select the invoice issue date.

After filling in all required fields, tap **Continue**.

<img width="173" height="350" alt="Payment details screen with fields for invoice number, authentication code, amount, and issue date." src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d05c992f-6b11-4d66-8b35-a800beb6471e" />

## Step 13: Confirm the details

Review the information displayed on the screen.

If the bank account is incorrect, tap **Use a different account**.
If the beneficiary is incorrect, select the correct beneficiary.

After confirming the information, tap **Submit**.

<img width="173" height="350" alt="Request confirmation screen showing beneficiary data, bank account, and the Submit button." src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/009960a4-6e04-4cd7-bf39-eef042b03f5e" />

## Step 14: Finish

After submitting the request, the app will display the protocol number and the deadline for completing the review.

Save the protocol number for future reference.

The reimbursement request has been completed.

<img width="173" height="350" alt="Request completion screen showing the protocol number and review deadline." src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/73d035b5-b7a5-49f7-a43c-721688ce9a53" />
