## UNIMED Seguros Chatbot: A Proposal for KPI Maximization with Precision and Effectiveness

**AI-powered chatbots in healthcare** are becoming one of the main enablers of digital transformation in the sector. When designed with a focus on **precision, safety, and patient experience**, they directly impact operational, financial, and care-related indicators.

### 24/7 Service and Operational Cost Reduction

Chatbots allow patients to get information, submit requests, and initiate service at any time, reducing reliance on staff for recurring demands.

**Impacted KPIs:**
- Operational cost reduction
- Reduction in CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)
- Increased service capacity
- Reduction in manual service volume

### Scheduling and Patient Journey

Automating appointments, confirmations, rescheduling, and pre-consultation guidance reduces friction along the patient journey and improves medical schedule utilization.

**Impacted KPIs:**
- Schedule occupancy rate
- No-show reduction
- Average scheduling time
- Patient satisfaction

### Response Time Reduction

By instantly answering frequently asked questions, the chatbot eliminates service queues and reduces the time needed to resolve simple requests.

**Impacted KPIs:**
- Average Handling Time (AHT)
- Average Response Time (ART)
- First Contact Resolution (FCR)
- NPS (Net Promoter Score)

### Intelligent Triage and Routing

Chatbots based on validated protocols and flows can collect initial patient information and direct them to the most appropriate channel, specialty, or care level.

**Impacted KPIs:**
- Correct referral rate
- Triage accuracy
- Reduction of inappropriate consultations
- Better distribution of care demand

### Team Productivity

By taking over repetitive and administrative tasks, the chatbot frees healthcare professionals and service teams to focus on higher-value, more complex demands.

**Impacted KPIs:**
- Operational productivity
- Care team efficiency
- Reduction of rework
- Better use of resources

### Patient Experience and Retention

A fast, intuitive, and accessible experience increases patient trust and strengthens the relationship with the institution.

**Impacted KPIs:**
- NPS
- CSAT (Customer Satisfaction Score)
- Patient retention rate
- Churn reduction

:::tip
**Key Success Factor:** A healthcare chatbot only generates value when it combines **high response accuracy**, **integration with clinical and administrative systems**, **regulatory compliance**, and an **intuitive conversational experience**. When these elements work together, the institution can scale service, increase operational efficiency, and significantly improve the patient journey.
:::

# The Ideal Chatbot for Unimed Seguros

## Beyond a Service Channel

The Unimed Seguros chatbot should not be treated as a tool for answering questions, but as an intelligent digital channel capable of accompanying the customer throughout their entire journey — from enrollment to the use of health services.

The main mistake of most corporate chatbots is being built around business logic. The ideal chatbot must be built around user logic.

Customers don't think in terms of "open a request," "check authorization," or "access services." They think:

- I need to schedule an appointment.
- I want to know if my plan covers this exam.
- Where can I find a nearby hospital?
- I need a duplicate bill.
- How do I add a dependent?
- I need to use my insurance now.

The ideal chatbot must immediately understand these intentions and guide the user to the outcome with the fewest possible interactions.

---

## Strategic Pillars

### 1. Digital Health Concierge

The chatbot should act as a personal health and insurance assistant.

Instead of just answering questions, it should proactively guide the customer throughout their journey.

Examples:

- Appointment reminders.
- Policy renewals.
- Upcoming bill due dates.
- Profile updates.
- Preventive campaigns.
- Request tracking.

Goal:
Transform reactive service into a continuous relationship.

---

### 2. Hyperpersonalization

After authentication, all responses should take into account:

- Plan type.
- Policyholder profile.
- Usage history.
- Geographic region.
- Linked dependents.

Example:

Instead of:

"Check the accredited network."

The chatbot responds:

"I found 12 accredited laboratories near your location. The highest-rated ones are X, Y, and Z."

Goal:
Increase perceived value and reduce user effort.

---

### 3. First Contact Resolution (FCR)

The chatbot's success should be measured by its ability to resolve the need without a human transfer.

Priorities:

1. Resolve.
2. Direct.
3. Escalate.

Never:

1. Ask.
2. Ask again.
3. Transfer.

Goal:
Maximum resolution with minimum friction.

---

### 4. Conversational AI, Not Rigid Flows

The user should be able to write naturally.

Examples:

- "I want to schedule a dermatologist."
- "My bill is overdue."
- "Where can I get an MRI?"
- "Is my son still on the plan?"
- "I lost my insurance card."

The AI should automatically interpret intentions and entities.

Goal:
Eliminate excessive menus.

---

### 5. Unified Ecosystem

The chatbot should centralize access to:

#### Care Services

- Accredited network
- Coverage inquiry
- Medical guides
- Telemedicine
- Authorizations
- Reimbursements

#### Administrative Services

- Bills
- Duplicate documents
- Profile updates
- Adding dependents
- Contracts
- Request status

#### Commercial Services

- Plan upgrades
- New coverage options
- Complementary products

Goal:
A single entry point for all services.
